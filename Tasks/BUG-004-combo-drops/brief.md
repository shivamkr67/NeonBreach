# BUG-004 — Combo drops even though I keep killing

**Reported by:** Player feedback

I'm killing an enemy every second or so, but my COMBO counter resets after a couple of seconds anyway and I never
reach the 2x score multiplier. If I kill something every second the combo should keep going until I stop for a bit.

**Expected:** the combo only resets if I go longer than the combo window (2.5 s) without a kill.
**Actual:** it resets about 2.5 s after the *first* kill of the chain, no matter what.
