# BUG-001 — Dash makes me untouchable

**Reported by:** QA (playtest session 14)

After I dash, enemies can walk up and hit me and nothing happens for a long time — my HP bar doesn't move
and there's no damage flash. It feels like I get a free "god mode" every time I use the dash. The dash itself
should only protect me for a split second while I'm actually dashing.

**Expected:** invulnerability ends right after the dash (about a quarter of a second).
**Actual:** I stay invulnerable for roughly as long as the dash cooldown.

Please fix the issue without changing the dash cooldown, speed or distance.
